Terms of Use

Last updated: May 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using TBH, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use this app.

2. Your Content

You are solely responsible for messages you send. Do not send harmful, threatening, or illegal content. TBH reserves the right to remove content that violates these terms.

3. Privacy

TBH uses a peer-to-peer architecture. Messages are sent directly between peers. We do not store your messages on a central server.

4. Prohibited Use

You may not use TBH for harassment, bullying, spam, or any illegal activity. Violations may result in account termination.

5. Disclaimer

TBH is provided "as is" without warranty. We are not responsible for content sent by other users.

6. Changes

We may update these Terms at any time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the new Terms.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

TBH only stores your username and app preferences locally on your device using localStorage. We do not have a central database of users.

2. Peer-to-Peer Messaging

Messages are sent directly between users via WebRTC (PeerJS). The PeerJS signaling server is only used to establish connections — your message content is never seen by us.

3. No Tracking

We do not use analytics, cookies, or any third-party tracking tools.

4. Data Deletion

You can delete all your local data at any time via Settings → Delete account. This immediately clears all stored data from your device.

5. Contact

Questions? Email us at privacy@tbh.app
